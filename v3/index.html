Questions at the counter.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. ZeroWait works with your business WhatsApp number, so customers keep messaging the number they already have.

Will customers know it's an assistant?

Yes. It introduces itself as your assistant and passes the chat to you whenever a customer needs you personally.

Can customers stop the messages?

Yes. Customers can reply STOP at any time and they will not receive further reminders or follow-ups.

Is customer data safe?

We only collect what's needed to book and remind, such as name, phone number and appointment time, and use it only for your business. Read our Privacy Policy.

How long does setup take?

We do it for you. Share your working hours and services, and you can be live within a day.
